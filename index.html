Maximizing Transfer Speed

1. Use Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot (Fastest: 20–50 MB/s)

If both phones are on 5G cellular data without Wi-Fi, carrier NAT throttles uploads to ~400 KB/s. Turn on Personal Hotspot on one phone and connect the other phone to it—no internet data is consumed!

2. Keep Turbo Mode Enabled for Large Videos

WebRTC already encrypts every packet with DTLS 1.2/1.3 and CRC32C checksums. Keeping Turbo Mode ON avoids extra JavaScript hashing overhead on multi-gigabyte 1080p/4K movies.

3. Keep Both Screens Open

Mobile OSes throttle background browser tabs. Keep LocalDrop in the foreground on both phones until the transfer reaches 100%.
