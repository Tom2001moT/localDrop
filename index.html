How to Hit 1 Gbps+ Speeds

1. Understand 1 Gbps vs 1 GB/s

1 Gbps (Gigabit/sec) = 125 MB/s, achievable over Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 or Gigabit Ethernet. 1 GB/s (Gigabyte/sec = 8 Gbps) requires 10GbE wired Ethernet or Thunderbolt networking.

2. Use 5 GHz / 6 GHz Wi-Fi or Hotspot (Never 2.4 GHz or 5G Data)

2.4 GHz Wi-Fi caps out at ~10–15 MB/s, and cellular data caps at ~0.5–2 MB/s. Switch your Personal Hotspot to 5 GHz ("Maximize Compatibility" OFF on iPhone) or use a Wi-Fi 6 router.

3. Turn Off Bluetooth Audio/Scanning During Huge Transfers

Phones share antenna time between Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Turning off Bluetooth during multi-gigabyte video transfers prevents radio airtime slicing.
