Preventing Drops & Boosting Speed

1. Keep Both Phones on Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot

Mobile carrier 5G/LTE networks drop peer-to-peer UDP bindings after 30–60 seconds. Using the same Wi-Fi router or one phone's 5 GHz Hotspot keeps transfers local and uninterrupted.

2. Keep the Browser Tab in the Foreground

iOS Safari and Android Chrome suspend background tabs after ~30 seconds to save battery. Keep LocalDrop open on screen until the transfer reaches 100%.

3. Disable Low Power Mode on Large Videos

Low Power / Battery Saver mode disables Screen Wake Lock and throttles Wi-Fi antenna power on mobile devices.
